Adjustment Rules
  • Follow the miss. Ball finishes left of the pocket → move feet and eyes left. Finishes right → move right.
  • 1-and-1 (parallel): feet and eyes move the same number of boards. Same angle, for small misses (1–2 boards at the pins).
  • 2-and-1: feet move 2 for every 1 with your eyes. Changes the angle as well as the position: use it when the lane changes, not for one bad shot.
  • One change at a time, then give it two good shots before judging it.
  • Not just feet: ball change, speed ±0.5 mph (faster = later hook), or less rotation in the hand (straighter).
  • Unsure? Put your line in Trajectory and tap Apply Recommended Line.

League Night: Game by Game

Game 1: Fresh oil · Viking Conquest

  • Start at 28 at the foul line, eyes 15. Keep speed at 16.5 mph and the release relaxed.
  • Pocket → stay put. Use the first 2–3 frames to learn the lane, not to chase it.
  • As the track dries, the ball starts hooking earlier and hitting high. Move left, usually 2-and-1, to stay ahead of it.

Game 2: Transition · switch to Zen Soul

  • Trigger: the Viking hooks early and dies. Weak hits (flat 10, 8-10, 5-7), or high hits even after moving left.
  • Don't force the solid by throwing it farther right or lofting it. Put it away.
  • Start the Zen Soul at 30 at the foul line, eyes 15. Throw one good shot, then follow the miss. Don't stack a ball change and a big move before seeing a shot.

Game 3: Late night · two things can happen

  • Fronts keep drying (ball still hooks early / high) → keep moving left and deeper with 2-and-1 moves.
  • Carrydown (oil pushed down the lane: ball skids long, hits light, won't come back) → move right 1-and-1, or go back to a stronger ball (Viking, or Storm Lock if it's really slick), or slow down slightly.
  • Tell them apart by where the ball goes wrong: early and high = dry; late and light = carrydown.

Spare System (TZone)

Eyes always on the center arrow (board 20). Only your mark at the foul line moves. Flat hand, no rotation, walk straight to your target.

PinFoul LineEyes
  • Multi-pin leaves: line up for the pin closest to you, e.g. 3-6-10 → shoot the 3-pin line nudged half a board toward the 6, or switch to your strike ball for big clusters.
  • Corner pins (7, 10): trust the cross-lane angle. Most misses come from drifting or turning the hand, not the line.
  • Splits: take the sure pin unless the pins are side by side.

Straight lines from the foul-line board through board 20 at the arrows to each pin.
